02 / Wall systems

What is behind a stucco wall

Repair cost comes down to which layer failed and how far the damage travelled. Select a layer, and switch between the two systems used on Alberta homes.

Outside Inside

    FactorTraditional stuccoEIFS
    Build upSand, cement, lime and water over wire lath, in three coatsRigid foam board, mesh reinforced base coat, thin acrylic finish
    Upfront costLower material cost, higher labourHigher, from insulation board and specialised install
    InsulationNone on its ownContinuous foam layer, real energy saving
    Impact and hailDense and rigid, resists hail wellSoft substrate, dents and punctures
    MoistureSlightly porous, trapped moisture can escapeNeeds a drainage cavity. Older barrier systems trap water
    WoodpeckersRarely targetedSounds hollow, so a frequent target

    If your home has EIFS: confirm any repair uses a modern drainage style assembly rather than an outdated barrier system. A patch that seals water inside the wall causes more damage than the hole it covered.

    03 / Damage guide

    Work out what you are looking at

    The six things we are called out for most. Compare what is on your wall against the reference photographs below, then send us the closest match.

    Cracks that grow each spring

    Meltwater enters a hairline crack, freezes overnight and expands by about nine per cent, levering the crack wider from the inside.

    Cause
    Freeze and thaw cycling
    Act
    Before the next winter

    Cracks at window corners

    A chinook can lift the temperature twenty degrees in an hour. The whole wall expands and contracts, and the corner of an opening is the weakest point.

    Cause
    Rapid temperature swing
    Act
    Monitor, repair if it widens

    Pockmarks on one elevation

    Hail between golf ball and tennis ball size is normal here from June through August. It chips acrylic finishes and punctures EIFS foam.

    Cause
    Hail impact
    Act
    Inspect after any major storm

    Dark streaks below windows

    Gusts over eighty kilometres an hour drive rain sideways into joints, flashing and window heads, where gravity would normally carry it clear.

    Cause
    Wind driven rain, failed flashing
    Act
    Investigate promptly

    Round holes high on the wall

    An EIFS wall is foam over an air gap, so it sounds hollow when tapped. To a woodpecker that reads as a dead tree.

    Cause
    Woodpecker activity on EIFS
    Act
    Repair and deter together

    White powder near ground level

    Efflorescence is mineral salt carried out of the wall by moving water. Cleaning it removes the mark, not the cause. Where it appears tells you where water is entering.

    Cause
    Moisture moving through masonry
    Act
    Find the source first

    Reference photographs are illustrations of each damage type, not photographs of our own projects.

    03a / Demonstration

    Why patching a woodpecker hole does not work

    A live EIFS wall, three real layers deep. Watch it open up, then try each repair. Click anywhere on the wall to move the bird.

    Holes opened
    0
    Depth reached
    Intact

    Watch the wall, or use the controls.

    The bird is a Northern Flicker, the species responsible for most stucco damage on the prairies. It is not after insects in the foam. It is drilling a nest cavity, and it chose your wall because the hollow sound matches a dead tree.

    That is why a cosmetic patch fails. The wall sounds identical afterwards, so the bird returns, usually within a few metres of the original hole. The repair has to rebuild the foam and mesh and change what the wall tells the bird.

    04 / Process

    How a job runs

    Stage two matters most. A repair that treats the symptom rather than the cause tends to return within a year or two.

    1. 01

      Assessment

      Free on site visit. We read the crack pattern, check flashing, caulking and gutters, and probe for moisture behind the finish.

    2. 02

      Diagnosis

      Drainage, flashing, foundation movement or original installation. We identify which before quoting.

    3. 03

      Written quote

      Scope, materials, timeline and payment schedule on paper before work begins. Free, no obligation.

    4. 04

      Preparation

      Loose material removed to sound substrate, damaged sheathing repaired, surfaces cleaned so new work bonds.

    5. 05

      Application

      Base coat, mesh and finish in sequence with proper cure times, using materials rated to minus forty.

    6. 06

      Match and clean

      Colour and texture matched by fog coat or tinted acrylic. Site cleaned daily, then walked with you.

    07 / Questions

    Common questions

    How much does stucco repair cost?
    It depends on the size of the damaged area, the type of stucco system, how accessible the elevation is, and how far the damage extends behind the finish. An on site assessment is the only accurate way to price it, and that assessment is free.
    Can cracked stucco be repaired, or does it need replacing?
    Most cracks and localised damage can be repaired. Which makes more sense depends on the cause and how widespread it is. If the same cause has been at work for several seasons, the damage behind the finish is usually larger than it looks from outside.
    Do you offer free quotes?
    Yes. Quotes are free, given in writing, and carry no obligation. The written quote sets out scope, materials, timeline and payment schedule before any work starts.
    Can you repair woodpecker damage to EIFS?
    Yes. Damaged foam and mesh are cut back and rebuilt rather than filled, and deterrents are fitted at the same time. A repaired wall still sounds hollow, so without deterrents the bird generally returns within a few metres of the original hole.
    How quickly can you respond after a hailstorm?
    Storm related enquiries are prioritised. We can usually schedule an inspection within a few days of a major weather event, depending on demand across the region at the time.
    Do you work on acreages and rural properties?
    Yes. Acreages are common across Rocky View, Foothills and Mountain View counties, and our crews are equipped for larger rural properties including sheds, outbuildings and extended exterior surfaces.
    When is the best time of year to book?
    Calgary has a shorter window for exterior work than most of Canada, because cement based products need temperatures above a threshold to cure correctly. Booking early in the season is the difference between having the work done this year and waiting for next.
    What should I ask any exterior contractor?
    Ask for proof of liability insurance and WCB coverage. Ask how much of their work has been in Calgary conditions, and whether they have experience with both traditional stucco and EIFS. Ask to see recent local work. Require a written quote listing scope, materials, timeline, payment schedule and warranty. Ask how they will diagnose the cause rather than only patching the symptom.
