What goes wrong, and what it means

  • Cracks that grow each springWater in small cracks freezes and expands. Repair the cracks before next winter.
  • Cracks at window cornersFast temperature changes put pressure on weak spots around windows. Watch the crack. Repair it if it grows.
  • Pockmarks on one elevationHail can chip the coloured outer coat. It can also punch holes in the foam behind it. Check the wall after a major storm.
  • Dark streaks below windowsWind-driven rain can get past damaged metal strips above windows. It can also get through gaps around windows. Have dark streaks checked promptly.
  • Round holes high on the wallWoodpeckers often target foam-backed stucco because it feels hollow. Replace damaged foam and reinforcing fabric. Add reflectors to keep the bird away.
  • White powder near ground levelWhite salt powder on concrete or brick means water is moving through the wall. Find where the water comes from before cleaning the mark.

The Northern Flicker causes most stucco damage on the prairies. It is not looking for insects in the foam. It is making a nest. The hollow wall feels like a dead tree.

That is why filling the surface fails. The wall still feels hollow. The bird often returns near the first hole. A full repair replaces the foam and reinforcing fabric. It also changes how the wall feels to the bird.

A‑06Process
Sequence

How a job runs

Finding the cause matters most. A repair that only covers the visible damage often fails within a year or two.

  1. 01

    Assessment

    We visit your home for free. We study the cracks. We check the metal strips above windows, the seals around them and your gutters. We also check whether water got in behind the surface.

  2. 02

    Diagnosis

    We find the cause. It may be poor water flow, damaged metal strips, foundation movement or earlier work. Then we quote.

  3. 03

    Written quote

    Your quote lists the work, materials, schedule and payment dates. It is free. There is no obligation.

  4. 04

    Preparation

    We remove loose material until only firm, undamaged layers remain. We repair damaged wood panels. We clean each surface so new material sticks.

  5. 05

    Application

    We put on each coat in order. We let each one harden before the next. The materials are rated to minus forty.

  6. 06

    Match & clean

    We match the colour and texture so the repair blends in. We clean the site daily. We inspect the finished work with you.

Questions

Straight answers

How much does stucco repair cost?
Four things affect the price. These are the size of the damaged area, the wall type, access to the damage and how far it has spread behind the surface. We cannot judge hidden damage from a photograph. We visit first. The assessment is free.
Can cracked stucco be repaired, or does the whole wall need redoing?
Most cracks can be repaired. Replacing the whole wall is better only when the cause has damaged a larger area behind the surface. This often happens after several seasons. We tell you which option you need before you decide.
Do you charge for quotes?
No. The visit is free. The assessment is free. The written quote is free. There is no obligation. The quote lists the work, materials, schedule and payment dates. This makes it easier to compare quotes.
Can woodpecker damage to EIFS actually be fixed?
Yes, but filling the hole is not enough. We cut back to firm material. We replace the foam and reinforcing fabric. Then we fit reflectors to keep the bird away. Skipping that step is why many repairs fail. A repaired wall still feels hollow. The bird may return near the first hole.
How fast can you come out after a hailstorm?
We give storm damage priority. We can usually inspect within a few days. A storm across the whole region keeps every contractor busy. Call early instead of waiting.
Do you work on acreages and rural properties?
Yes. We regularly work across Rocky View, Foothills and Mountain View counties. We work on houses, sheds, garages and other buildings.
When should I book?
Book earlier than most people expect. Cement products need warm enough weather to harden correctly. Alberta has a shorter season for outside work than most of the country. When that season is full, the next opening is the following year.
What should I ask any contractor before hiring them?
Ask five questions. Ask us the same ones.
  • Proof of liability insurance. Proof of provincial workplace injury insurance.
  • How much work they have done through Calgary freezing and thawing. Ask whether they work with traditional and foam-backed stucco.
  • Recent local homes you can visit and see.
  • A written quote listing the work, materials, schedule, payment dates and warranty.
  • How they will find the cause instead of hiding the damage.
